# Rally Software Announces Pricing of Initial Public Offering
## Share this article
BOULDER, Colo. , April 12, 2013 /PRNewswire/ -- Rally®, a leading provider of cloud-based solutions for managing Agile software development, announced today the pricing of its initial public offering of 6,000,000 shares of its common stock at a price to the public of $14.00 per share.
The shares are expected to begin trading on the New York Stock Exchange on April 12, 2013 , under the symbol "RALY." In addition, the company has granted the underwriters a 30-day option to purchase up to 900,000 additional shares of common stock.
