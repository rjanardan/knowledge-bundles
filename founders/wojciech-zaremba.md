# Wojciech Zaremba

A co-founder of OpenAI who led its robotics team, then its GPT, Copilot and Codex teams, and who now leads AI resilience at the OpenAI Foundation.[^openai-intro] [^wiki-zaremba]

## Snapshot

* Born 30 November 1988 in Poland, and a silver medallist at the 48th International Mathematical Olympiad.[^wiki-zaremba]
* Co-founder of OpenAI, named in the December 2015 founding announcement.[^openai-intro]
* Led OpenAI's robotics work from 2016, including the robotic hand that solved a Rubik's cube.[^wiki-zaremba]
* Since the robotics team was dissolved in 2020 he has led teams working on OpenAI's GPT models, GitHub Copilot and Codex.[^wiki-zaremba]
* Reported in April 2026 as leading AI resilience at the OpenAI Foundation, covering biosecurity, child safety and model safety.[^foundation-news]

## Education

* Studied at the University of Warsaw and Ecole Polytechnique; BSc in mathematics in 2010, then a BSc in computer science and an MSc in mathematics in 2012, all at Warsaw.[^wiki-zaremba]
* PhD at New York University in deep learning, supervised by Yann LeCun and Rob Fergus, graduated 2016, with time split between Google Brain and Facebook AI Research.[^wiki-zaremba]

## Career and outcomes

* Interned at Nvidia as an undergraduate, and co-authored work on adversarial examples for neural networks during his time at Google.[^wiki-zaremba]
* Co-founded OpenAI in December 2015 and moved through robotics, then the model teams, then the coding products.[^openai-intro] [^wiki-zaremba]
* The OpenAI Foundation, which controls the group, committed at least $1B a year from 2026 across disease research, AI safety and community programmes, with him leading the resilience strand.[^foundation-news]

## Research contributions

* Adversarial examples for neural networks, a result that reframed robustness as a security property rather than a benchmark.[^wiki-zaremba]
* The robotics-to-models transition inside OpenAI, including the widely covered cube-solving hand and, later, the coding-agent line.[^wiki-zaremba]
