# Sam Altman

## Snapshot

* Born in Chicago on 1985-04-22; attended Stanford for two years and left without graduating to co-found Loopt.[^wiki-altman]
* Co-founder of OpenAI in December 2015 and its chief executive since March 2019, after leaving Y Combinator.[^wiki-altman]
* Removed as chief executive by the OpenAI board on 2023-11-17 and reinstated five days later after employee and investor pressure produced a new board.[^wiki-altman]
* Personally an investor in more than 400 companies, with the portfolio valued at about $2.8B as of June 2024, including Reddit, Worldcoin, Helion Energy and Instacart.[^wiki-altman]

## The 2023 removal and reinstatement

The board removed Altman on 2023-11-17, citing a lack of confidence in him and stating that he had not been consistently candid in his communications.[^wiki-altman] The move produced a backlash from employees and investors; about 738 of OpenAI's roughly 770 employees signed an open letter saying they would leave and join Microsoft, and the board that had removed him was replaced within five days.[^wiki-openai] Altman has since said he considered starting a new company and taking former OpenAI employees with him if talks had failed, and he insisted that board members who supported the removal resign; they agreed in principle to do so.[^wiki-openai]

The reasons given later differ in emphasis from the original statement. In May 2024 the former board member Helen Toner said the board's concern was that Altman had withheld information, giving as an example that the board was not told in advance about the launch of ChatGPT.[^wiki-altman] This bundle records both accounts and does not reconcile them.

## Investor

As of June 2024 Altman's portfolio contained stakes in over 400 companies valued at around $2.8B, and some of those investments intersect with companies doing business with OpenAI, which has raised conflict-of-interest questions; OpenAI's board chairman Bret Taylor said Altman had been transparent about the investments.[^wiki-altman]

## Litigation

Elon Musk filed suit against OpenAI and Altman in February 2024, and in April 2026 revised the claim to seek reversal of OpenAI's corporate restructuring; he testified from 2026-04-30, and on 2026-05-18 a federal jury found for OpenAI, Altman, Greg Brockman and Microsoft on the ground that Musk had missed the filing deadlines. His attorney announced an appeal.[^wiki-openai]
