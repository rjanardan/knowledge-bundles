# Pamela Vagata

A founding member of OpenAI who had already built Facebook's company-wide machine learning platform, later Stripe's AI engineering lead, and since September 2021 a co-founder of the seed-stage fund Pebblebed.[^openai-intro] [^pebblebed-team]

## Snapshot

* Named in the December 2015 founding announcement among OpenAI's founding members.[^openai-intro]
* At Facebook she created FBLearner Flow, the machine learning platform that became the backbone of AI across the company.[^pebblebed-team]
* A founding member of OpenAI, where she helped establish the engineering foundation, and later the leader of AI engineering at Stripe.[^pebblebed-team]
* Co-founder and managing partner of Pebblebed, a seed-stage venture firm for technical founders that describes itself as making about twenty investments per fund.[^pebblebed] [^milken]

## Career and outcomes

* Registered as a founding partner of Pebblebed from September 2021, in San Francisco.[^vcsheet]
