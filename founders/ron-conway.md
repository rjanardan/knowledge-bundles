# Ron Conway

An operator at three semiconductor and software companies who became the most prolific individual angel in San Francisco, and whose funds were early in Google, PayPal and Loudcloud before he turned his personal vehicle into SV Angel.[^wiki-conway]

## Snapshot

* Born 9 March 1951 in San Francisco, into an Irish Catholic family; graduated from San Jose State University with a bachelor's degree in political science.[^wiki-conway]
* Marketing at National Semiconductor from 1973 to 1979, president and chief executive of Altos Computer Systems from 1988 to 1990, and chief executive of Personal Training Systems from 1991 to 1995, which was acquired by SmartForce and SkillSoft.[^wiki-conway]
* Began angel investing in the mid-1990s, raised 4 million dollars for a first fund, Adam Ventures, in 1997, and founded Angel Investors LP in December 1998.[^wiki-conway]
* Angel Investors LP was an early investor in Google, Ask Jeeves, Loudcloud, Napster and PayPal, which is the fact that made his name; a 2006 Forbes Midas list entry recognised it.[^wiki-conway]
* A special partner at Baseline Ventures from 2006 to 2009, then in 2009 he turned his personal investment vehicle, SV Angel, into a venture firm with 10 million dollars from outside investors.[^wiki-conway] [^svangel]
* SV Angel raised six funds through 2018, when he announced a return to investing as individual angels; in March 2022 the firm announced a 269 million dollar growth fund led by Ashvin Bachireddy, with Beth Turner leading the seed fund and Ron and Topher Conway as managing co-directors.[^wiki-conway]
* He has made more than 650 investments and joined the Giving Pledge in 2021.[^wiki-conway]

## Education

* BA in political science from San Jose State University. No graduate degree is recorded in the sources read.[^wiki-conway]

## Career and outcomes

* His operating career ran from semiconductor marketing through two chief executive roles, one of them at Altos Computer Systems in the years it was a significant microcomputer maker.[^wiki-conway]
* The three funds form a sequence rather than three unrelated facts: the personal dealings of the mid-1990s, then Adam Ventures with 4 million dollars, then Angel Investors LP, which raised 30 million dollars for its first fund within two months of founding and 150 million dollars for its second at the end of 1999.[^wiki-conway]
* In September 2010 he publicly criticised the private investor meetings that became known as Angelgate, and the same period produced the criticism he took for his own partner's attendance at them.[^wiki-conway]
* The 2018 change of strategy reduced cheque sizes and reverted to investing his own money; the 2022 growth fund reversed that with outside capital.[^wiki-conway]

## Contributions
