# Vicki Cheung

The founding engineer who built OpenAI's early infrastructure, after an early stint at Duolingo and before a decade of infrastructure and platform engineering elsewhere.[^openai-intro] [^kubecon]

## Snapshot

* Named in the December 2015 founding announcement among OpenAI's founding members.[^openai-intro]
* Head of infrastructure and founding engineer at OpenAI, per her own speaker biography for KubeCon Europe 2020.[^kubecon]
* Before OpenAI, an early engineer at Duolingo, which she describes as growing from a team of four to forty, where she led the Incubator platform and built the Android app and website.[^cheung-site]
* Later a staff software engineer at Lyft, where she helped drive the company-wide Kubernetes migration, and an engineering lead at TrueVault.[^kubecon] [^cheung-site]
* Her own professional profile describes her current work as angel investing and advising, with a vice presidency of engineering at Harmonic among the earlier roles.[^linkedin-cheung]
