# DST Global

A late-stage fund with an unusual origin: it was carved out of Digital Sky Technologies, the Russian internet group that became Mail.ru Group and later VK, to hold the parent company's international investments.[^wiki-dst] It has no portfolio page and no published fund sizes after 2015, which makes the two AI positions this file can source unusually valuable - both of them come from the investee's side of the table.[^tc-ssi][^tc-anthropic-h]

## Snapshot

* **Founded.** 2009 by Yuri Milner as a separate fund management company for international investments, spun out of Digital Sky Technologies.[^wiki-dst]
* **Co-founders.** Saurabh Gupta, John Lindfors, Rahul Mehta and Tom Stafford.[^wiki-dst]
* **Offices.** Menlo Park and New York, among others.[^wiki-dst]
* **What it invests in.** Late-stage internet companies, a mandate set in the early 2010s when the firm's international money went into emerging markets such as China.[^wiki-dst]
* **Disclosure style.** The composition of shareholders and the final size of the 2015 fifth fund are described in the encyclopaedia entry as unknown.[^wiki-dst]

## Timeline

* **1999.** Milner founds Digital Sky Technologies, the parent that becomes Mail.ru Group.[^wiki-dst]
* **2009.** DST Global is set up as the dedicated vehicle for international investments.[^wiki-dst]
* **2011-01.** Partners with SV Angel to launch the Start fund, which invests in every Y Combinator startup with an offer of 150,000 dollars in convertible debt.[^wiki-sva]
* **2015-08.** The fifth fund has raised 1.7 billion dollars, with shareholders undisclosed.[^wiki-dst]
* **2024-09-04.** Named among the five investors in the reported 1 billion dollar round in Safe Superintelligence.[^tc-ssi]
* **2026-05-28.** Listed among the institutional investors in the Anthropic Series H.[^tc-anthropic-h]
