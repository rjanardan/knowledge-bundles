# a16z

Andreessen Horowitz, universally shortened to a16z, is the Menlo Park firm that turned from super-angel investing into the largest venture capital operation of its generation.[^wiki-a16z] For this bundle it matters for a specific reason: it is one of the five investors named in the Safe Superintelligence round, and it holds positions across the coding, voice, model and robotics layers at the same time.

## Snapshot

* **Founded.** July 2009 by Marc Andreessen and Ben Horowitz, with 300 million dollars of initial capitalisation.[^wiki-a16z]
* **Headquarters.** Menlo Park, California.[^wiki-a16z]
* **Scale.** 90 billion dollars in assets under management as of January 2026, after raising 15 billion dollars across new funds that month.[^wiki-a16z]
* **General partners named in the encyclopaedia entry.** Marc Andreessen, Ben Horowitz, Jeff Jordan, Chris Dixon, Martin Casado and Andrew Chen, with Raghu Raghuram joining in October 2025.[^wiki-a16z]
