# Mira Murati

OpenAI's chief technology officer during the ChatGPT period and its interim chief executive for three days, and since February 2025 the co-founder and chief executive of Thinking Machines Lab.[^wiki-murati]

## Snapshot

* Born 16 December 1988; Albanian.[^wiki-murati]
* **Not a founder of OpenAI.** She joined in 2018 as vice president of applied AI and partnerships, two and a half years after the December 2015 founding, and the founding announcement does not name her.[^wiki-murati] [^openai-intro]
* Chief technology officer of OpenAI from May 2022 to September 2024, and interim chief executive for three days in November 2023, replaced by Emmett Shear.[^wiki-murati]
* Co-founder and chief executive of Thinking Machines Lab, launched in February 2025 as a public benefit corporation.[^wiki-murati] [^tml-site]

## Education

* Attended Pearson College UWC on Vancouver Island on a scholarship, graduating in 2007 with an International Baccalaureate.[^wiki-murati]
* BA from Colby College in 2011 and a BEng in mechanical engineering from Dartmouth College in 2012, through a dual-degree programme.[^wiki-murati]

## Career and outcomes

* Summer analyst at Goldman Sachs in Tokyo in 2011, then Tesla in 2013 as a product manager on the Model X.[^wiki-murati]
* Led product and engineering at Leap Motion from 2016, before joining OpenAI in 2018.[^wiki-murati]
* Left OpenAI in September 2024 amid a wider executive exodus that also took the chief research officer and a vice president of research.[^wiki-murati]
* Launched Thinking Machines Lab in February 2025, hiring about thirty researchers and engineers from Meta, Mistral and OpenAI, with advisors including Alec Radford and Bob McGrew.[^wiki-murati]
* Thinking Machines Lab's first product, Tinker, was announced in October 2025; in 2026 the lab shipped open-weights models, Inkling on 15 July and Inkling-Small on 30 July, and published research grants in interactivity and safety.[^wiki-murati] [^tml-news]

## Research contributions

* Her role is organisational rather than published: the applied-AI and product surface at OpenAI through the ChatGPT period, and now a lab that ships open weights.[^wiki-murati] [^tml-news]

## Public positions

* Said in June 2024 that some creative jobs may disappear, qualifying that such roles were questionable where the resulting content was of low quality; the remark drew criticism from writers, artists and Dartmouth students.[^wiki-murati]
* Received an honorary Doctor of Science from Dartmouth College in June 2024.[^wiki-murati]
