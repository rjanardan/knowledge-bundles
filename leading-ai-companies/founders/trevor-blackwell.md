# Trevor Blackwell

A roboticist and Y Combinator partner named in December 2015 as one of the founding members of OpenAI, and the founder of the humanoid robotics company Anybots.[^openai-intro] [^wiki-blackwell]

## Snapshot

* Born 4 November 1969 in Canada, grew up in Saskatoon, Saskatchewan, and is based in England.[^wiki-blackwell]
* A developer of humanoid robots; founder and former chief executive of Anybots and a partner at Y Combinator.[^wiki-blackwell]
* Named in the founding announcement among OpenAI's founding members.[^openai-intro]
* No current artificial intelligence role is established in the sources read for this file, which is a gap rather than a finding.[^wiki-blackwell]

## Education

* BEng at Carleton University in 1992, then a PhD in computer science at Harvard University in 1998.[^wiki-blackwell]

## Career and outcomes

* Joined Viaweb during graduate school, writing its image rendering, order processing and statistics software - the company that became Yahoo Store.[^wiki-blackwell]
* Founded Anybots in 2001 to build teleoperated humanoid robots.[^wiki-blackwell]
* Became a Y Combinator partner, the role through which he appears in the same professional circle as several other early OpenAI backers.[^wiki-blackwell]

## Research contributions

* Teleoperated humanoid robotics, predating the current wave of embodied-AI work by two decades.[^wiki-blackwell]
