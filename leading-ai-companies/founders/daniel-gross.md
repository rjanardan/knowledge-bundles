# Daniel Gross

The youngest founder Y Combinator had accepted when he went through it, then a machine-learning director at Apple, then a venture investor; the thread that matters here is that he co-founded Safe Superintelligence with Ilya Sutskever, ran it as chief executive, and left in July 2025 for Meta Superintelligence Labs.[^wiki-gross]

## Snapshot

* Born in Jerusalem in 1991; Israeli-American, raised Orthodox and expecting a religious life in Israel before he applied to Y Combinator while attending a pre-army mechina.[^wiki-gross]
* Launched Greplin, a personal search engine, in 2010 with Robby Walker, at the time one of the youngest founders Sequoia Capital had backed; the company renamed itself Cue in 2012 and Apple acquired it in 2013, for a figure reported between 40 and 60 million dollars.[^wiki-gross]
* Stayed at Apple as a director focused on machine learning until 2016.[^wiki-gross]
* A Y Combinator partner in 2017 and 2018, where he built the programme's AI track, and the creator in August 2018 of Pioneer, a remote accelerator and fund.[^wiki-gross]
* Co-founded AI Grant with Nat Friedman in 2017 and NFDG with him in 2023; the same pair deployed the Andromeda Cluster, 2,512 H100 GPUs costing around 100 million dollars, for their portfolio companies in 2023.[^wiki-gross] [^aigrant]
* Co-founded Safe Superintelligence in June 2024 with Ilya Sutskever and Daniel Levy and served as its chief executive, leaving in July 2025 for Meta Superintelligence Labs.[^wiki-gross] [^wiki-msl] [^tc-ssi]
* Named to the Time 100 list of the most influential people in AI in 2023.[^wiki-gross]

## Education

* The reference entry read here records no university degree. It records the mechina, the Y Combinator batch he entered from it, and the angel investing he began in 2011.[^wiki-gross]

## Career and outcomes

* Greplin was designed to let users search across their own online accounts, and the pivot to Cue added predictive search; it raised 10 million dollars from Index Ventures in November 2012.[^wiki-gross]
* His Apple period is the origin of the artificial intelligence framing other files in this bundle use for him: machine learning, inside a company that was then buying rather than building in that area.[^wiki-gross]
* At Y Combinator he concentrated on artificial intelligence and created the AI programme; Pioneer was aimed at founders outside the usual pipeline.[^wiki-gross]
* Through NFDG the pair invested across the frontier: the fund's file records the positions this bundle can evidence, and the fund holds a stake in Safe Superintelligence, the company he co-founded.[^wiki-gross]
* In February 2025 he and Friedman invested 3.9 million dollars in Pulse, and in June 2025 Meta sought to acquire Safe Superintelligence and was refused. Gross left the following month.[^wiki-gross]

## Contributions

* He is an operator and allocator rather than a publishing researcher: a search product, machine-learning work at Apple, a Y Combinator programme, an accelerator, a supercomputer cluster run for portfolio companies, and two funds.[^wiki-gross]
* The Andromeda Cluster is the concrete contribution: compute bought for startups rather than for a lab, reported at 4,000 GPUs by 2024, and offered to open-source projects through AI Grant.[^wiki-gross] [^aigrant]
