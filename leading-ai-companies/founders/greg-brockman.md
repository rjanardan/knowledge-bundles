# Greg Brockman

OpenAI's chief technology officer at its founding and its president since, and one of the two authors of the announcement that started the lab.[^openai-intro] [^wiki-brockman]

## Snapshot

* Born 29 November 1987 in Thompson, North Dakota.[^wiki-brockman]
* Co-founder and president of OpenAI.[^wiki-brockman]
* Began his career at Stripe in 2010 on leaving MIT, and became Stripe's chief technology officer in 2013.[^wiki-brockman]
* Left Stripe in 2015 to co-found OpenAI, where he served as CTO.[^wiki-brockman]
* Estimated net worth of $25.5B in July 2026, almost all of it his OpenAI stake.[^wiki-brockman]

## Education

* Studied at MIT and left before completing a degree, beginning at Stripe in 2010.[^wiki-brockman]

## Career and outcomes

* Co-authored the December 2015 founding announcement with Ilya Sutskever and the OpenAI staff; the same post names him as CTO, "formerly the CTO of Stripe".[^openai-intro]
* Remained through the November 2023 board crisis as one of the executives the employee letter defended, and is named in the resulting litigation.[^wiki-openai]
* On 18 May 2026 a federal jury decided in his favour along with Altman and OpenAI, finding Musk's claims barred by the statute of limitations.[^wiki-brockman]

## Research contributions

* His contributions are organisational rather than published: the engineering organisation and the product cadence that turned a research lab into a consumer and enterprise platform.[^wiki-brockman]

## Public positions

* Was, with his wife, the largest donor to the MAGA Inc. Super PAC in 2025, at $12.5M each.[^wiki-brockman]
* Helped found Leading the Future, an AI deregulation Super PAC, with Marc Andreessen and Ben Horowitz, and gave it $50M; OpenAI has said publicly that it is open to increased regulatory oversight and has a policy against donating to such committees.[^wiki-brockman]
