# Nat Friedman

A developer-tools executive who spent two decades on free software and hosting before becoming one of the more visible private investors in frontier artificial intelligence, and who has led product at Meta Superintelligence Labs since 2025.[^wiki-nat] [^wiki-msl]

## Snapshot

* Born 6 August 1977; American.[^wiki-nat]
* Co-founded Ximian in 1999 with Miguel de Icaza; Novell bought the company in 2003 and kept him as chief technology and strategy officer for open source until January 2010.[^wiki-nat]
* Founded Xamarin in May 2011, again with de Icaza, as its chief executive. Microsoft acquired it in 2016.[^wiki-nat]
* Chief executive of GitHub from October 2018 until 2021, and a former chairman of the GNOME Foundation.[^wiki-nat]
* Co-founded AI Grant with Daniel Gross in 2017, and the investment partnership NFDG with him in 2023.[^aigrant] [^wiki-nat]
* Head of product at Meta Superintelligence Labs, the division Meta founded in June 2025 and put under Alexandr Wang.[^wiki-msl] [^dcd-nfdg]
* A board member of the Arc Institute and an advisor to Midjourney.[^wiki-nat]

## Education

* Graduated from St. Anne's-Belfield School in 1995.[^wiki-nat]
* Studied computer science and mathematics at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology from 1995; the sources read do not state a degree.[^wiki-nat]
* Met Miguel de Icaza in 1996 on LinuxNet, the IRC network Friedman had created to discuss Linux. The friendship produced Ximian, then GNOME support work under Novell, then Xamarin.[^wiki-nat]

## Career and outcomes

* Interned at Microsoft as an undergraduate, on the IIS web server.[^wiki-nat]
* Ximian was built around GNOME, the free-software desktop that de Icaza had started; after the Novell acquisition Friedman ran open source strategy there and launched the Hula Project, which released components of Novell NetMail as open source.[^wiki-nat]
* Xamarin existed to carry Mono, the free-software implementation of Microsoft's .NET stack, and to sell the cross-platform SDK to developers targeting mobile devices and consoles. Microsoft's 2016 acquisition returned the project to the owner of the framework it reimplemented.[^wiki-nat]
* At GitHub he ran the company through the period in which Microsoft's ownership settled and Copilot moved from research demonstration to product.[^wiki-nat]
* Since the middle of 2025 he has been part of the leadership of Meta Superintelligence Labs, whose model family is now Muse, including Muse Spark, released in July 2026.[^wiki-msl]

## Contributions

* His career is infrastructure rather than published research: a free-software desktop, a cross-platform .NET runtime, and developer hosting.[^wiki-nat]
* AI Grant, which he created with Gross, funds open-source AI work with grants of 10,000 to 100,000 dollars and with compute on the Andromeda Cluster; the programme states that more than thirty grants have been given since 2017.[^aigrant]
* The same programme offers 250,000 dollars on an uncapped SAFE to AI-native startups, plus 350,000 dollars in Azure credits, and runs a three-day summit in San Francisco.[^aigrant]
