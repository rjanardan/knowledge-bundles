# Elon Musk

Co-chair of OpenAI from its founding until he resigned from the board in 2018, after which he became its most vocal critic and then its litigant.[^openai-intro] [^wiki-openai]

## Snapshot

* Born 28 June 1971; Wikipedia describes him as a businessman and a former public official.[^wiki-musk]
* Chief executive and largest shareholder of Tesla and SpaceX, the wealthiest person in the world since 2025, and briefly the only dollar trillionaire, in June 2026.[^wiki-musk]
* Co-chair of OpenAI at its founding, alongside Sam Altman.[^openai-intro]
* Resigned from the OpenAI board in 2018.[^wiki-openai]
* Launched xAI in July 2023, explicitly to compete with the company he had helped found.[^wiki-musk]

## Career and outcomes

* Co-founded Zip2 in 1995; co-founded X.com in 1999, which merged with Confinity in March 2000 to form PayPal, acquired by eBay in 2002.[^wiki-musk]
* Co-founded OpenAI in December 2015 and, with Altman, put in his own money into the $1B that the founding group pledged.[^openai-intro]
* Founded Neuralink in 2016 and the Boring Company in 2017.[^wiki-musk]
* Launched xAI in July 2023, hiring engineers from Google and OpenAI and funding it with money from investors in SpaceX and Tesla.[^wiki-musk]
* In 2026 xAI became a subsidiary of SpaceX, and the company's news page now carries the SpaceXAI name.[^wiki-musk] [^xai-news]
* Sued OpenAI and its executives in February 2024 over the founding agreement; on 18 May 2026 a jury in Oakland decided against him, finding the claims barred by the statute of limitations.[^wiki-musk] [^wiki-openai]

## Research contributions

* None in the published sense; his contribution to OpenAI was capital, recruitment and public profile, and to xAI it is capital and compute politics.[^wiki-musk] [^openai-intro]

## Public positions

* His stated objection to OpenAI is that it departed from its founding agreement by prioritising profit over safety, which is the claim the 2026 jury rejected on limitation grounds rather than on the merits.[^wiki-openai]
* Launched Grokipedia in October 2025 as an AI-generated alternative to Wikipedia; it effectively stopped updating in April 2026.[^wiki-musk]
