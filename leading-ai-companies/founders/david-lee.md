# David Lee

The managing partner of SV Angel through its formative years, whose abrupt departure from it in 2015 was the most public split in seed investing of that decade, and who has led Samsung's venture arm since 2021.[^wiki-lee]

## Snapshot

* Born on 7 July; the year is not stated in the sources read. American.[^wiki-lee]
* Bachelor's degree from Johns Hopkins University, master's from Stanford University, Juris Doctor from New York University.[^wiki-lee]
* Joined Google in 2003 as a founding member of its new business development team, then invested at Steve Anderson's Baseline Ventures before joining Ron Conway's SV Angel in 2007.[^wiki-lee]
* Managing partner of SV Angel from 2007 until May 2015; in 2012 Conway announced publicly that the firm was "David's fund".[^wiki-lee]
* Named to the Forbes Midas list for 2014 and 2015.[^wiki-lee]
* Co-founded Refactor Capital in 2016 with Zal Bilimoria, formerly a partner at Andreessen Horowitz.[^tc-refactor] [^finsmes-refactor]
* Head of Samsung Next and an executive vice president of Samsung Electronics since 2021.[^samsung-next]

## Education

* Johns Hopkins University, Stanford University and New York University, for a bachelor's degree, a master's degree and a law degree respectively. The subjects are not stated in the sources read.[^wiki-lee]

## Career and outcomes

* His Google period was on the business development side rather than the engineering side, which is the usual misreading of the "former Google executive" line that follows him in press coverage.[^wiki-lee]
* He was a participant in the 2010 private investor meetings that became the Angelgate affair, and Conway publicly criticised his attendance at them while Conway was his partner.[^wiki-lee]
* In December 2012 he moved from Silicon Valley to Los Angeles while remaining managing partner, which the reference entry records as a period of unusual arrangement for a sole managing partner.[^wiki-lee]
* The departure in May 2015 was announced "effective immediately" in what the reference entry describes as a terse memo with no quote from Lee, after Conway had told limited partners that Lee had breached his trust, principally over pay above the firm's 250,000 dollar annual salary cap for a managing partner.[^wiki-lee]
* Refactor Capital followed in March 2016, disclosed first through an SEC filing and reported as targeting 50 million dollars; the firm closed its maiden fund at that size.[^tc-refactor] [^finsmes-refactor]
* He has served on the board of the Lucile Packard Foundation for Children's Health.[^wiki-lee]

## Contributions

* His contribution to this corpus is the seed model at SV Angel in the years when it invested as a two-partner firm, and the record of what that model cost when it broke: the file is in part a case study in key-person risk at a small fund.[^wiki-lee]
* At Samsung Next he runs a corporate venture arm, which is the category this bundle treats as a company node rather than as an `investors/` concept, per the scope note in the conventions.[^samsung-next]
