# Amjad Masad

## Snapshot

* Born 1987/1988 in Amman, Jordan; studied computer science at Princess Sumaya University for Technology.[^wiki-amjad]
* Serial route through developer education: built JSRepl in 2011, then a founding engineer at Codecademy, then Facebook's JavaScript infrastructure team, then Replit from 2016.[^wiki-amjad]
* Co-founder and CEO of Replit; the company reached a $9B valuation in March 2026.[^techcrunch-replit-d]
* His co-founders are his brother Faris Masad and his wife Haya Odeh, so the Replit founder cluster is a family cluster - relevant to any founder-graph analysis, because it means three nodes move together.[^wiki-amjad]

## Founding story and product thesis

The line from JSRepl to Replit is continuous: an in-browser interpreter that removes installation entirely, then a hosted development environment, then an agent that writes the code in the same environment. The current version of that thesis, in his own investors' words, is "prompt-to-production" - the claim that a person with an idea can generate, deploy and iterate on software in one place.[^georgian-series-d] Replit Agent, the product that carries it, first shipped in September 2024.[^wiki-replit]

He also states that angel investors in Replit's Series D include Shaquille O'Neal and Jared Leto, a detail reported from his own post on X rather than from a filing.[^techcrunch-replit-d]
