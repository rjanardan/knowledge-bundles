# Demis Hassabis

## Snapshot

* Born 1976-07-27 in London to a Greek Cypriot father and a Chinese Singaporean mother.[^wiki-hassabis]
* Chairman and co-founder of Google DeepMind, chief scientist of Alphabet from 2026, chief executive and co-founder of Isomorphic Labs, and a UK Government AI Adviser.[^wiki-hassabis]
* Joint winner, with John M. Jumper, of the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for AI research contributions to protein structure prediction.[^wiki-hassabis]

## Three careers before the lab

Hassabis's track record is unusual in this bundle because the first two careers are not in AI research at all, and both are documented well enough to cite.

| Game development | Completed A-levels two years early at 16; took a gap year at Bullfrog Productions at Cambridge's request because of his age, worked with Peter Molyneux, and turned down a seven-figure sum to stay in the industry, using his earnings to fund university | [^wiki-hassabis] |

| Neuroscience | PhD in cognitive neuroscience from UCL's Queen Square Institute of Neurology in 2009, supervised by Eleanor Maguire, after postdoctoral work at MIT in Tomaso Poggio's lab and at Harvard, and a Henry Wellcome postdoctoral fellowship at the Gatsby Computational Neuroscience Unit with Peter Dayan | [^wiki-hassabis] |
| The lab | Co-founded DeepMind in November 2010 with Shane Legg, whom he met as a fellow postdoc at the Gatsby unit, and Mustafa Suleyman, a friend through family; served as chief executive until 2026, when he became Alphabet's chief scientist | [^wiki-hassabis] [^wiki-deepmind] |

## Why the sequence matters for this bundle

The through-line the sources support is that Hassabis recruited and led a lab whose research identity is reinforcement learning and games, and then turned that capability on scientific problems: AlphaGo, AlphaZero and MuZero on one side, AlphaFold on the other, with the Nobel Prize following the protein work.[^wiki-deepmind] The bundle therefore records him with edges to a game studio and to a university neuroscience department as well as to the lab, because a founder's prior domain is part of the graph that the brief calls success-rate tracking.

## Recognition

Appointed CBE in 2017 and knighted in 2024 for his work on AI; a Fellow of the Royal Society; named to the Time 100 in 2017 and 2025; and one of the "Architects of AI" collectively named Time's 2025 Person of the Year, alongside the Breakthrough Prize, the Canada Gairdner International Award and the Lasker Award.[^wiki-hassabis]
