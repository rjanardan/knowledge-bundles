# Ilya Sutskever

OpenAI's research director at its founding, later its chief scientist, and since 2024 the co-founder and chief executive of Safe Superintelligence Inc.[^openai-intro] [^wiki-sutskever]

## Snapshot

* Born in 1986 in Nizhny Novgorod, then Gorky, in the Soviet Union. The family moved to Israel when he was five and to Canada when he was sixteen, where he was admitted to the University of Toronto as a third-year undergraduate after a month of high school.[^wiki-sutskever]
* Named in the December 2015 founding announcement as research director, and described there as one of the world experts in machine learning.[^openai-intro]
* Co-founder and chief executive of Safe Superintelligence Inc since June 2024.[^wiki-sutskever]
* Net worth estimated at $13.8B by the Bloomberg Billionaires Index in July 2026, most of it an OpenAI stake he verified on the stand in the 2026 Musk v. Altman trial.[^wiki-sutskever]

## Education

* BSc mathematics 2005, MSc computer science 2007 and PhD computer science 2013, all at the University of Toronto, with Geoffrey Hinton as doctoral advisor.[^wiki-sutskever]
* Spent about two months as a postdoc with Andrew Ng at Stanford in 2012, then returned to Toronto and joined Hinton's company DNNResearch.[^wiki-sutskever]

## Career and outcomes

* Co-founded OpenAI in December 2015 as research director, later chief scientist, and left in 2024.[^openai-intro] [^wiki-sutskever]
* Sat on the board that removed Sam Altman in November 2023; Hinton publicly supported that decision a year later.[^wiki-sutskever]
* Co-founded Safe Superintelligence Inc in June 2024 with Daniel Gross and Daniel Levy. The company's own site calls it "the world's first straight-shot SSI lab", with one product and one goal, a safe superintelligence.[^wiki-sutskever] [^ssi-site]
* SSI rejected a Meta acquisition approach in June 2025, and Sutskever became chief executive after Gross left for Meta.[^wiki-sutskever]
* Nvidia announced a $5B investment in SSI in July 2026.[^wiki-sutskever]

## Research contributions

* AlexNet, with Alex Krizhevsky and Geoffrey Hinton, in 2012.[^wiki-sutskever]
* Sequence-to-sequence learning, and contributions to CLIP, DALL-E and AlphaGo.[^wiki-sutskever]
* The GPT model line and reasoning models, as the research lead of the lab that produced them.[^wiki-sutskever]

## Public positions

* SSI's stated position, which is also his, is that safety and capability are one technical problem rather than two, approached "in tandem" through engineering and scientific breakthroughs.[^ssi-site]
* Received an honorary doctorate from the University of Toronto in 2025 and the National Academy of Sciences Award for the Industrial Application of Science in 2026, the first time that award was given for artificial intelligence.[^wiki-sutskever]
