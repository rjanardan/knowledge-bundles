# Andrej Karpathy

A founding member of OpenAI, its research scientist twice over, Tesla's AI director in between, the founder of an AI-native school, and since May 2026 a pretraining researcher at Anthropic.[^openai-intro] [^wiki-karpathy]

## Snapshot

* Born 23 October 1986 in Bratislava, then Czechoslovakia, and moved to Toronto at fifteen.[^wiki-karpathy]
* Founding member of OpenAI, a research scientist there from 2015 to 2017.[^wiki-karpathy]
* Tesla's director of artificial intelligence from June 2017, reporting to Elon Musk, until July 2022.[^wiki-karpathy]
* Founded Eureka Labs, an AI education company, on 16 July 2024.[^eureka]
* Since 19 May 2026 he has been at Anthropic, leading a team for research in pretraining.[^wiki-karpathy]

## Education

* BSc in computer science and physics at the University of Toronto in 2009, and an MSc at the University of British Columbia in 2011.[^wiki-karpathy]
* PhD at Stanford in 2015 under Fei-Fei Li, on the intersection of natural language and vision; authored and taught the first deep learning course there, CS 231n, which grew from 150 students in 2015 to 750 in 2017.[^wiki-karpathy]

## Career and outcomes

* Co-founded OpenAI in December 2015 as one of the research engineers named in the founding announcement.[^openai-intro]
* Left for Tesla in June 2017; after a sabbatical, left Tesla in July 2022.[^wiki-karpathy]
* Returned to OpenAI on 9 February 2023 and left again on 13 February 2024.[^wiki-karpathy]
* Announced Eureka Labs on 16 July 2024, "building a new kind of school that is AI native".[^eureka]
* In February 2025 he coined the term "vibe coding" for building software by prompting.[^wiki-karpathy]
* Announced on 19 May 2026 that he had joined Anthropic, which said he would lead a team for research in pretraining.[^wiki-karpathy]

## Research contributions

* Deep learning for vision and language, and one of the most widely used teaching artefacts in the field, the CS 231n course and its lecture series.[^wiki-karpathy]
* The vocabulary of the current wave, through "vibe coding", a term that by 2026 was in use far outside the field.[^wiki-karpathy]

## Public positions

* His public position on AI has been consistently educational rather than apocalyptic: understand the systems, build with them, teach the building.[^eureka]
