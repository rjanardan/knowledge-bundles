# Safe Superintelligence

A lab with a name that is also its entire roadmap. SSI was founded in June 2024 by Ilya Sutskever after he left OpenAI, and it is the node where this directory's new investors overlap: four of the five funds written alongside this file appear on no other investment together.[^tc-ssi]

## Snapshot

* **What it is.** An American company with offices in Palo Alto and Tel Aviv, describing itself as "the world's first straight-shot SSI lab, with one goal and one product: a safe superintelligence".[^ssi-site]
* **Founded.** Announced on 19 June 2024 by Sutskever, with Daniel Gross and Daniel Levy.[^wiki-ssi]
* **Valuation.** 30 billion dollars after the March 2025 round led by Greenoaks Capital, six times the 5 billion dollar valuation of the previous September.[^wiki-ssi]
* **Revenue.** None. The company is described as not generating revenue and having roughly 20 employees at the time of the 2025 round.[^wiki-ssi]
* **Chief executive.** Sutskever, since the co-founder Daniel Gross left in July 2025.[^wiki-ssi]

## Timeline

* **2024-05-15.** Sutskever leaves OpenAI after the board dispute in which he voted to remove Sam Altman.[^wiki-ssi]
* **2024-06-19.** He announces the new company with Levy and Gross.[^wiki-ssi]
* **2024-09-04.** The reported 1 billion dollar round is disclosed, naming NFDG, a16z, Sequoia, DST Global and SV Angel, at a 5 billion dollar valuation.[^tc-ssi]
* **2025-03.** A round led by Greenoaks Capital takes the valuation to 30 billion dollars.[^wiki-ssi]
* **2025-04.** Google Cloud agrees to provide TPUs for the lab's research.[^wiki-ssi]
* **2025-07.** In the first half of the year Meta tries to acquire the company and is refused; Gross leaves for Meta Superintelligence Labs and Sutskever becomes chief executive.[^wiki-ssi]
* **2026-07-27.** A strategic partnership with Nvidia is announced, expanding compute by an order of magnitude and including a planned 5 billion dollar investment.[^wiki-ssi]

## Founding and team

Three names, and the sources agree on all three.[^wiki-ssi] Sutskever is the former chief scientist of OpenAI; Gross is described as the former head of Apple's artificial intelligence efforts and, at the time of the 2024 round, as the company's chief executive; Levy is described as an investor and former OpenAI researcher.[^wiki-ssi][^tc-ssi]

The split between the two cities is stated by the company itself, which describes "deep roots and the ability to recruit top technical talent" in both Palo Alto and Tel Aviv.[^ssi-site]

Sequoia's portfolio page lists the same three people under team, with Andrew Reed and Shaun Maguire as the partners on the account, and dates the partnership to the year of founding.[^seq-ssi]

## Research background

The company publishes a mission and no papers. What it states about method is a sequencing claim rather than a technical one: safety and capabilities are approached "in tandem, as technical problems", with the intention to "advance capabilities as fast as possible while making sure our safety always remains ahead".[^ssi-site]

## Models and products

None shipped. The firm's own framing is that there is no separate product to ship: "SSI is our mission, our name, and our entire product roadmap".[^ssi-site] The sibling concept [Thinking Machines Lab](/companies/thinking-machines-lab.md) took the opposite route, shipping an interactive product within a year of founding.

## Technology stack

Not disclosed. The only stack fact established across the sources is the compute supply: Google Cloud TPUs from April 2025 and the Nvidia partnership from July 2026.[^wiki-ssi] No source read states model architecture, parameter counts or training data for anything the lab has built.

## Contributions to the ecosystem

The clearest contribution is a structural one: SSI is the proof that a frontier lab can raise at a 30 billion dollar valuation with no product, no revenue and a headcount near 20, on the reputation of a single researcher.[^wiki-ssi] It is also, as of this batch, the most connected pending target in the bundle - which is why the concept was written before the funds that point at it.
