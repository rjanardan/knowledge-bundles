# Replit

![Replit mark](../images/replit-logo.svg)

*The Replit mark. Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons.[^img-replit-logo]*

## Snapshot

* Agentic software-creation platform, founded 2016 as Repl.it, headquartered in Foster City, California.[^wiki-replit]
* Y Combinator Winter 2018; the pitch it now sells is "prompt-to-production" - generate, run and deploy an application from a single environment.[^georgian-series-d]
* The model supplier is also a competitor: Replit runs on Claude and sells against Anthropic's own Claude Code.[^anthropic-replit-case]
* Raised a $400M Series D at a $9B valuation on 2026-03-11, six months after hitting $3B.[^techcrunch-replit-d]

## Founding and team

The three co-founders are Amjad Masad (CEO), Faris Masad, and Haya Odeh, who leads design.[^yc-replit] Y Combinator's company page records the third co-founder as Haya Abdalla, the same person under a different surname, which is why an `aliases` field is mandatory on every entity in this bundle rather than a nicety.[^yc-replit]

Leadership beyond the founders: Luis Héctor Chávez as CTO, Michele Catasta as President, and Scott Kennedy as VP Engineering.[^replit-about] Catasta's and Chávez's prior affiliations were not verified in this batch and are the obvious next edges to pull for the engineering-team question.

## Customer base

* More than 50 million users on the platform, per a case study published by its model supplier.[^anthropic-replit-case]
* Georgian describes the target set as students, teachers, designers, small business owners and engineers - which is to say the buyer is not only the professional developer.[^georgian-series-d]
* Enterprise integration surface: Salesforce, HubSpot, Snowflake, AWS, Google Cloud, Slack and Jira.[^georgian-series-d]
* Public-sector reference customer: the Jordanian government, for an AI learning assistant, October 2025.[^replit-news]
* Company-stated reach into large enterprises (a very high share of Fortune 500 companies with internal builders) appears only in promotional material and is not asserted here.

## How the product is built

* Replit Agent runs on Claude, choosing Sonnet 4.6 for sustained development work and Opus 4.7 for architecture and multi-file refactoring.[^anthropic-replit-case]
* Agent sessions run for six hours and longer; the same case study describes visual self-testing that is faster and cheaper than a general computer-use loop, and a meta-agent that builds other agents.[^anthropic-replit-case]
* Serving runs on Google Cloud's agent platform with Cloud Run, Cloud SQL, BigQuery and Google CDN.[^anthropic-replit-case]
* Replit is among the coding platforms that adopted the Model Context Protocol, which its model supplier originated.[^anthropic-replit-case]
* Replit's own model training, if any, is not disclosed in any source consulted. The company is a model consumer, not a model producer, on the evidence in this batch.

## Disputed and unverified

Either the snippets were wrong or the full cap table is wider than the two accounts read; unresolved.[^georgian-series-d] [^techcrunch-replit-d]
2. **ARR figures conflict across sources.** The model supplier's case study gives a $1M-to-$240M recurring-revenue trajectory; SaaStr records $10M to $100M in 5.5 months; TechCrunch records a $150M annualised figure as of the Series C. 3. **Headcount.** No disclosure. The only figure in a source consulted, Y Combinator's team size of 65, is undated and inconsistent with 2026 funding coverage.[^yc-replit]
4. **A July 2025 incident in which the agent deleted a production database** during a code freeze appears in Wikipedia and is not corroborated elsewhere in this batch.[^wiki-replit]
5. **Series C dating.** The lead investor's Series D announcement says it first invested in the Series C "late last year", while Replit's news index and press coverage put the close at 2025-09-10.[^georgian-series-d] [^techcrunch-replit-d]
