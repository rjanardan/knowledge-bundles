# Anthropic

![Anthropic wordmark](../images/anthropic-logo.svg)

*The Anthropic wordmark. Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons.[^img-anthropic-logo]*

## Snapshot

* Frontier model lab, founded January 2021 by former OpenAI research staff, incorporated as a Delaware public benefit corporation and headquartered in San Francisco.[^wiki-anthropic]
* Sells the Claude model family through a first-party API, subscription products, and all three major clouds; AWS is the primary cloud and training partner.[^anthropic-series-h]
* Originated the Model Context Protocol in November 2024 and donated it to the Linux Foundation in December 2025, which is the single largest source of outward edges from this company.[^wiki-mcp]
* Raised $65B at a $965B post-money valuation on 2026-05-28, described in press coverage as potentially its last private round before listing.[^techcrunch-series-h]

## Founding and team

The founding team came out of OpenAI, and the roster as recorded in Wikipedia's infobox runs to eight names: Dario Amodei (CEO), Daniela Amodei (President), Tom Brown, Jack Clark, Jared Kaplan, Chris Olah, Sam McCandlish and Ben Mann.[^wiki-anthropic] The same source's prose describes the founding group as seven former OpenAI employees, and this batch could not resolve which count is right; see [Disputed and unverified](#disputed-and-unverified).

Daniela Amodei is President and runs operations; she was previously Vice President of Operations at OpenAI.[^wiki-daniela] The pair hired Jan Leike and John Schulman away from OpenAI in 2024.[^wiki-anthropic] Krishna Rao is Chief Financial Officer.[^techcrunch-series-h]

## Research background

The lab's research identity was set by three OpenAI-era threads its founders carried over: scaling of training compute against loss, reinforcement learning from human feedback, and mechanistic interpretability.[^wiki-anthropic] Reinforcement learning from human feedback is the technique Dario Amodei describes himself as a co-inventor of.[^dario-site]

**Claims deliberately not asserted in this batch.** Widely repeated but unsourced here, because no source consulted states them: authorship of the 2020 neural scaling laws paper, authorship of the GPT-3 paper, Chris Olah's role in the original circuits line of interpretability work, and which founders are named authors on the Constitutional AI paper. The bundle's rule is that a claim with no `sources` entry does not go in; these belong in iteration 2, against the papers themselves.

## How the frontier models are built

* **Constitutional AI** is the published method the lab is identified with. A written constitution replaces part of the human preference labelling that plain RLHF needs, with AI feedback supplying the rest. The constitution carried 75 guidelines in its 2023 form and ran to roughly 23,000 words in the 2026 revision.[^wiki-claude]
* **Capability tiering in production.** Replit uses Sonnet 4.6 for sustained development work and Opus 4.7 for architecture and multi-file refactoring, which is evidence that the intended split between fast and deep tiers is real in customer deployments rather than marketing.[^anthropic-replit-case]
* **Long-horizon agentic sessions.** Claude runs agent sessions of six hours and longer in Replit's product, and the same case study describes a meta-agent that builds other agents.[^anthropic-replit-case]
* **Not in this batch.** Pretraining corpus composition, mixture-of-experts use, distillation, parameter counts and training compute volumes are not disclosed by the company, and no source consulted states them. Those fields are left empty rather than filled by inference. This is the largest hole in the file and the first thing iteration 2 should attack.

## Contributions to the ecosystem

The Model Context Protocol is Anthropic's most consequential contribution by fan-out. Released 2024-11-25, it was created by Anthropic engineers David Soria Parra and Justin Spahr-Summers, and OpenAI adopted it in March 2025.[^wiki-mcp] In December 2025 Anthropic donated it to the Agentic AI Foundation, a directed fund under the Linux Foundation that Anthropic, Block and OpenAI co-founded.[^wiki-mcp] By mid-2026 the source records more than 10,000 public MCP servers and roughly 97 million SDK downloads per month, and a major revision on 2026-07-28 made the protocol stateless.[^wiki-mcp]

Agent Skills, published December 2025, is the second contributed standard.[^wiki-mcp]
